Privacy policy

This private app has one user, its owner.

With the owner's consent, it has read-only access to the owner's own Gmail account. It reads message headers, such as sender, subject and date. It cannot send, change or delete email.

The data is used only for the owner's personal purposes and is stored on a private server controlled by the owner. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

The owner can remove access at any time from their Google Account permissions page.
